Our Story

Built on hard work, one player at a time

Baka Skills Soccer Academy is a team founded by a hard-working man called Coach Kakande, whose aim is to develop young footballing talent across Uganda.

From training grounds to team tours, every activity is designed to sharpen ability and character together. Recently, the boys visited a grass cutter as part of the academy's ongoing tours — one more way our young stars pick up new skills off the pitch as well as on it.

Joining the academy means wishing your child the best in his career. We're here for you, every step of the way.

Founder

Coach Kakande

Head of Baka Skills Soccer Academy, committed to instilling skills, discipline, and hardwork into every young player who joins the Baka Army. His vision drives an academy that treats development as a full journey — not just a training session.
